🚀 Hackathon build: **FeeLure — "See the catch before you click."**

Ever reached the last step of a checkout and found the total quietly higher than the price you clicked on? Pre-ticked add-ons, late-disclosed handling fees, "free trials" that become subscriptions — these dark patterns cost consumers real money, and they're hard to spot in the moment.

For this hackathon I built **FeeLure**, a consumer-safety community portal that fights back:

🔍 **Report what you saw** — submit a site link, an optional screenshot, and a short description of the pattern you encountered (hidden fees, pre-checked add-ons, hidden subscriptions, difficult cancellation, misleading wording).

🤖 **AI-assisted first read** — an optional Google Gemini analysis of your screenshot highlights *potential* concerns: the exact visible evidence, the amount involved, what's uncertain, and a practical precaution. Everything is clearly labelled as a suspected pattern — you review and edit the AI's findings before anything is published. FeeLure never claims a company is doing anything illegal, and it never pretends to inspect live websites.

🗳️ **Community index** — real reports with search, category filters, and upvoting, so the clearest evidence rises to the top.

🛠️ Under the hood: Next.js 15 + React 19 + Tailwind, SQLite persistence, Gemini strictly server-side (your API key never touches the browser), graceful fallbacks when the AI is unavailable.

💡 The demo on the homepage shows exactly how it works with a fictional ₹999 → ₹1,247 checkout — pre-ticked ₹149 "protection plan" included.

Because trust online shouldn't require reading the fine print with a magnifying glass.
