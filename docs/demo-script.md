# FeeLure — 60-second demo script

**[0:00–0:08] Hook (Home page)**
> "This is FeeLure — see the catch before you click. That '₹999' deal? By the final step it's often ₹1,247. FeeLure is a community index that documents exactly how that happens."

**[0:08–0:20] The demo showcase (scroll to "See FeeLure in Action")**
> "Here's a fictional checkout: a ₹149 protection plan pre-ticked 'for you', and a ₹99 handling fee that only appears at the last step. On the right — the FeeLure analysis card: each concern, the exact evidence on screen, the amount, and one practical action: check your add-ons and the total before paying. It's labelled clearly: fictional example, not a real scan."

**[0:20–0:38] Submit + AI analysis (Submit Report)**
> "Reporting a real pattern takes a minute. Paste the URL, attach a screenshot, and — optionally — let Gemini take a first read. It only describes what's visible: suspected category, the exact wording, the amount, and what's uncertain. I review every finding, edit or discard anything wrong, then publish. FeeLure never visits the website and never accuses anyone of a crime — it records experiences and suspected patterns."

**[0:38–0:50] Community index + voting (Explore Reports)**
> "Published reports land in the community index — searchable, filterable by category, sortable by newest or most votes. Upvotes push the clearest evidence to the top. Everything is stored in a real database, so reports persist and every visitor sees the same index."

**[0:50–1:00] Close**
> "FeeLure — consumer safety, community powered, AI-assisted but human-verified. See the catch before you click."

*Delivery notes:*

- If the AI is slow/quota-limited during recording, use the demo showcase + a manually submitted report; the AI panel already exists in the recording of the review step.
